## Overview

Sunset Vista Condos brings the first purpose-built, full-amenity residential tower to **West End** — Roatán's most walkable and internationally recognized neighborhood. Located at the southern end of the paved West End road, the 10-story building places 95 residences within walking distance of the island's best diving, dining, and sunset views.

The project is developed by Bayside Inn in partnership with Econova Legacy, bringing institutional-quality construction and amenities to a neighborhood where inventory has historically been limited to small guesthouses and boutique hotels.

### Location

Sunset Vista Condos is positioned on the paved West End road, approximately 1.5 km south of the West End roundabout and 2 km from the West Bay Beach turnoff. The on-site parking structure (183 spaces across 3 levels) addresses West End's chronic parking constraints — a practical advantage for vacation rental income.

## Tomas's Take

West End has always been the heart of Roatán's international community, but until now it's been almost impossible to buy a new, professionally managed apartment there. Sunset Vista fills a real gap in the market.

**What I like:** The 1BR Type D units in the 69–70 m² range are the most interesting entry point for rental investors. West End's walkability and diving infrastructure drive year-round demand from short-term renters. The 183-space parking structure is genuinely rare in West End and will be a strong selling point for vacation rentals.

**What to consider:** Pricing has not yet been released publicly. The West End market has historically been less liquid than West Bay for resale, so buyers with a short (under 5-year) horizon should be aware of that. The building's contemporary urban aesthetic differs from the island's traditional vernacular — a plus for the target market, but worth knowing.
